Welcome! Have a seat, it's only the beginning!

Name's Astrid, I am a woman. You may have known me with another name/gender in the past, in the end, it doesn't matter, 
this past is no more.

I've been procrastinating for a long while (since early 2021) to bring this CMS up and running. Because of recent matters, 
I have decided to clean off the dust here and bring it online, once and for all!

I guess it is time for the usual presentation round,

As I said, name's Astrid, French native, no problems with english (this will be the only language of this blog, possibly 
some exceptions, we'll see), 26yo.

Education-wise, I have studied electronics then CompSci and finally CyberSec. I have (finally) finished these studies in 
2023 and sincerely hope I won't need to get back to school before a long while.

Like many people who studied CompSci, I like doing sysadmin tasks, for this reason, I have many of my services self-hosted or hosted with some friend's help  
I also _love_ breaking stuff. Because of this, last year, with a dear friend, we cleared _Dante_ from [HackTheBox](https://app.hackthebox.com/prolabs/overview/dante). 
If possible, I want to clear Rastalabs or Zephyr one day too...

Because of a lot of things, I happened to love and advocate towards Free Software, this world is a fascinating one
but can quickly become a huge mess.  
Because of this, I can definitely understand why people wants to stick with Microsoft and FAANG stuff. My stance is and 
will always be neutral, even if I do not like something, as long as someone is happy with it, it's fine.

Job-wise, I'm a sysadmin in a hosting company. You may consider why this job instead of breaking servers (pentester)?  
The answer is quite simple, as long as I suck at writing reports, I cannot conceive working on a pertest job.  
I love to present clean things, so, as long as my reports aren't clean, it's impossible to pursue a job in that field.

It's possible that I end up writing here whatever comes to mind, from personal to professional stuff.
